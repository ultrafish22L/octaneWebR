# Glass & Metal (Scene 1)

Three spheres on a bright white floor under daylight, showcasing different material types.

## Environment

Daylight environment at moderate power. Bright, clean, studio-like. Blue sky gradient visible in the background.

## Floor

A large white floor plane. Matte or very lightly reflective. Extends well beyond the spheres.

## The Spheres

Three spheres arranged in a row, left to right:

- **Gold metallic** (left) — Glossy gold material. Warm reflective surface catching the sky and other spheres.
- **Blue glass** (center, largest) — Specular glass with blue tint. High IOR. Shows the scene refracted through it with beautiful caustic shadows on the floor beneath.
- **Red matte** (right) — Diffuse red material. Smooth, solid, providing warm color contrast.

## Camera

Eye-level, centered on the glass sphere. Close enough to see detail in the caustics and reflections. Landscape orientation.

## The Look

A clean material showcase — gold catches warm highlights, glass refracts the world with caustic light patterns on the white floor, red provides a matte counterpoint. Bright, crisp, technically precise.
