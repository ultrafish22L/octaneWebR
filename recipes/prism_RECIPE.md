# The Prism (Scene 7)

A dark triangular glass prism splitting light into warm and cool color beams on a floor.

## Environment

Dark environment. Minimal ambient light — the scene is dramatic and focused.

## Floor

A reflective floor that catches the colored light beams from the prism. Shows warm tones (orange/yellow) on one side and cool tones (blue/cyan) on the other.

## The Prism

A triangular prism shape (prism.obj mesh). Dark specular glass — the glass itself appears nearly black, but transmits and refracts light through its faces. Positioned at center, resting on the floor.

## Lighting

Side lighting that enters one face of the prism and exits the other, splitting into spectral components. Warm orange/golden light emerges from one side, cool blue/cyan from the other. The prism acts as a classic Newtonian light splitter.

## Camera

Close-up, slightly above, angled to show both the warm and cool light beams emerging from the prism faces. The prism fills the center of the landscape frame.

## The Look

A Dark Side of the Moon homage. The dark glass prism sits quietly while splitting white light into warm and cool spectral bands. Orange and gold spill from one face, blue and teal from the other. Moody, minimal, iconic.
