# The Arch (Scene 13)

A sci-fi gateway — two tall gold pillars connected by a chrome ring at the top, with a glowing sphere floating at center.

## Environment

Starfield texture environment at low power (~1.5). Deep space backdrop providing subtle ambient fill and visible stars.

## Floor

A large dark mirror floor (floor.obj, scaled 10x). Glossy material — very dark diffuse, high specular, near-zero roughness. Reflects the arch structure and starfield.

## The Pillars

Two tall pillars (pillar.obj mesh) standing upright, positioned symmetrically left and right of center. Gold glossy material — warm gold diffuse, gold-tinted specular, low roughness. Scaled tall and narrow. The pillars serve as the vertical supports of the gateway.

## The Ring

A chrome ring (ring.obj mesh) positioned at the top of the pillars, connecting them horizontally. Chrome glossy — white/silver diffuse, bright specular, near-zero roughness. Scaled to span the gap between the pillars. Slightly tilted to face the camera.

## The Orb

A small emissive sphere (sphere_hd.obj) floating at the center of the arch, between and below the ring. Standalone diffuse material with blackbody emission — warm white, moderate power. The orb is the primary light source, illuminating the inner surfaces of the pillars and ring from within the gateway.

## Key Light

A quad light positioned high above and slightly in front of the arch (y=4, z=2), angled 70° down. Warm blackbody emission at moderate power, size 3. Provides the key illumination on the gold pillar surfaces. The orb supplements from within the gateway.

## Camera

Standing back at eye level (y=1), looking slightly upward into the arch. Offset just a touch to the right (x=0.3) for asymmetry. Far enough to capture the full arch — pillars, ring, orb, and floor — in a single wide frame. Landscape orientation (1000×563, 16:9).

## The Look

A gateway to the cosmos. Two gold pillars frame the view like ancient columns, connected by a gleaming chrome arch above. A warm glowing orb floats at the center, its light catching the gold surfaces and reflecting in the mirror floor. Stars visible through and around the structure. Monumental, sci-fi, reverent.
