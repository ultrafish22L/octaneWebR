# The Monolith (Scene 5)

A dark rectangular monolith standing atop a chrome glass sphere, on a light reflective floor.

## Environment

Soft, slightly dark environment. Gentle gradient lighting from the side, creating subtle shadows.

## Floor

A light, slightly reflective floor. Catches the sphere's caustics and the monolith's shadow.

## The Sphere

A large chrome/glass sphere (sphere mesh) sitting on the floor. Specular material with dispersion — rainbow chromatic rings visible in the refracted light. The sphere acts as a pedestal for the monolith above.

## The Monolith

A tall, narrow rectangular block (monolith.obj mesh) standing upright on top of the glass sphere. Dark matte material — nearly black. Stark, geometric, minimal. The monolith's reflection is visible distorted in the sphere below.

## Lighting

Side/back lighting that catches the sphere's surface and creates dispersion rings. The monolith remains dark and silhouetted.

## Camera

Slightly below center, looking up at the monolith-sphere composition. Centered framing, landscape orientation.

## The Look

A surreal, 2001-inspired composition. The dark monolith — austere, geometric, alien — stands impossibly balanced on a chromatic glass sphere. Rainbow dispersion rings appear where light enters the sphere. The contrast between the matte black rectangle and the prismatic sphere creates visual tension.
