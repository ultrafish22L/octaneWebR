# The Shrine (Scene 6)

An inverted glass cone floating above a brilliantly colorful reflective floor, splitting light into a full rainbow spectrum.

## Environment

Dark environment. The scene is lit entirely by a bright emission source above.

## Floor

A highly reflective floor that acts as a mirror for the rainbow light. The cone's dispersed light paints the floor in vivid spectral colors — greens, pinks, yellows, purples radiating outward.

## The Cone

An inverted cone/pyramid shape (prism.obj mesh) pointing downward. Specular glass with high IOR and dispersion. The cone splits the light from above into a full rainbow spectrum, projecting vivid colors onto the floor below and into the environment. The cone's surfaces show rainbow gradients — green, yellow, pink, purple.

## Lighting

A bright quad light directly above the cone. The light enters through the cone's flat top and disperses through the glass, creating a spectacular rainbow projection below.

## Camera

Low angle, looking up at the inverted cone from below. The rainbow floor fills the lower portion of the frame, the cone dominates the center, and the bright light source is visible above. Landscape orientation.

## The Look

Pure spectral magic. The glass cone acts as a giant prism, splitting white light into every color of the rainbow. The reflective floor becomes a canvas of dispersed color. Vivid, saturated, almost psychedelic — a celebration of chromatic dispersion in path tracing.
