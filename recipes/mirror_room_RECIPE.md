# The Mirror Room (Scene 8)

A large glass sphere in a dark enclosed room, lit from above, creating rainbow dispersion rings and infinite reflections.

## Environment

Completely dark/enclosed. No ambient light — only the area light above provides illumination.

## Room

Dark walls/enclosure around the sphere, visible as reflections in the glass surface.

## The Sphere

A large glass sphere (sphere mesh) filling most of the frame. Specular glass with high IOR and dispersion. The sphere shows vivid rainbow concentric rings — red, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta — created by chromatic dispersion of the overhead light. The room and light source are reflected and refracted through the glass with rainbow color separation.

## Lighting

A bright rectangular quad light directly above the sphere. Visible as a white rectangle reflected in the top of the sphere, with a rainbow halo around it from dispersion. The light creates concentric rainbow rings in the glass.

## Camera

Close-up, looking straight at the sphere from the front. The sphere dominates the frame. Square orientation (1:1 aspect ratio).

## The Look

A hypnotic glass orb. The overhead light creates perfect concentric rainbow rings through the glass — a physical demonstration of chromatic dispersion. The bright white light source reflects at the top, surrounded by vivid spectral halos. Mesmerizing, scientific, beautiful.
