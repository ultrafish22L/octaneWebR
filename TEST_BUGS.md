| BUG-R3-1    | High     | OPEN   | **Node max width too small for high-pin-count nodes**: Nodes like "Thin lens camera" (30 handles) and "Universal camera" (63 handles!) are clamped to `NODE_MAX_WIDTH = 240px` (base 320 × 0.75 scale).
